Archive

Upload tweets.js (or tweets.json) from your X data archive to fetch reposts older than what X's timeline allows. Only post IDs and dates are kept from the file; each post is then fetched with the same pacing and rate cap as the sync, newest first. Posts the sync already saved are skipped. If your archive is split into tweets-part1.js, tweets-part2.js…, upload each one.
